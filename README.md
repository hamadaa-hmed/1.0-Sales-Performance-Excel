# Superstore Excel Data Analysis Project

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an Excel-based data analysis and dashboard project focused on analyzing sales profitability across customers, products, categories, regions, and years.

The goal is to transform business data into meaningful insights that can support better understanding of profitability and business performance.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Dashboard Design

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard analyzes profitability from multiple perspectives:

- Total Profit by Year
- Profit by Region
- Top Customers by Profit
- Top Products by Profit
- Average Profit by Category
- Profit by Product Subcategory

## 💡 Key Insights

### Overall Performance

- Total profit analyzed: approximately **$2.39M**.
- Profit performance varies significantly across years, regions, products, and categories.

### 📅 Profit by Year

- **2012** recorded the highest total profit at approximately **$902K**.
- **2011** generated approximately **$703K**.
- **2013** generated approximately **$640K**.
- **2014** recorded approximately **$150K**, considerably lower than the previous years.

### 🌍 Profit by Region

The highest-profit regions/countries in the analysis included:

- **United Kingdom:** approximately $552K
- **Germany:** approximately $476K
- **France:** approximately $440K
- **Australia:** approximately $258K
- **Southwest:** approximately $318K

This shows that profitability is concentrated in several key markets.

### 🚲 Profit by Product

Road bikes generated the largest share of product profitability.

- **Road Bikes:** approximately $1.75M
- **Mountain Bikes:** approximately $617K
- **Touring Bikes:** approximately $21.5K

Among the individual products, **Road-150 Red, 62** generated approximately **$243K** in profit, making it the highest-profit product in the analyzed list.

### 👥 Top Customers

The analysis also identifies customers contributing the most profit.

- **James Hendergart:** approximately $11.38K
- **David Liu:** approximately $8.16K
- **Mike Taylor:** approximately $6.32K
- **Garth Fort:** approximately $6.07K
- **Felicia Gomez:** approximately $6.02K

### 🏷️ Average Profit by Category

- **Bikes:** approximately $2,453 average profit
- **Components:** approximately $239
- **Clothing:** approximately $38
- **Accessories:** approximately $23

This indicates a substantial difference in average profitability between product categories.

## 🎯 Business Takeaways

The analysis highlights several areas that can support business decision-making:

1. Focus on high-profit product lines, particularly Road Bikes.
2. Monitor the performance of major markets such as the United Kingdom, Germany, and France.
3. Investigate the significant decrease in profit observed in 2014.
4. Identify and retain high-value customers who generate significant profit.
5. Compare product categories to understand where profitability is concentrated.
